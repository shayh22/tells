Tales of the Mirror

Modern fables about emotional defences, the patterns couples fall into, and the road back to yourself

Seven fables, each one about a single pattern that quietly wears a relationship down. They are written to be recognised rather than studied: you read one, you see yourself somewhere in it, and you know what there is to repair.
